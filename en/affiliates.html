How it works

The program is simple and intentionally small: approval and follow-up are handled manually by us today, customer by customer.

1. You apply

Fill out the form below telling us who you are and how you plan to recommend Ledyvas (social media, your own customers, industry contacts, etc.).

2. We review your application

We review every application one by one and confirm by email — it's a real conversation, not a form that approves itself.

3. You introduce the customer

Let us know which business you'd like to refer (or the customer mentions your name when contacting us) — from there, the whole sale process is handled automatically.

4. You get paid

If the sale goes through, we pay you 10% net (on what Ledyvas actually receives) 15 days after the sale is confirmed.

Clear rules, no surprises

  • Single level. You earn 10% only on the customers you refer directly. There's no commission for affiliates who in turn bring other affiliates — it's not a multi-level scheme.
  • Net commission. The 10% is calculated on the amount left to Ledyvas after paying the taxes owed on that sale, not on the list price. Ledyvas doesn't offer a refund policy.
  • 15-day holdback. Payment is made 15 days after the sale is confirmed, to allow time for the sale to be fully closed out.
  • Close follow-up. Every application and every referred sale is reviewed one by one — there's no automatic tracking link yet, so we confirm everything by email.
  • No exclusivity, no cost. There's nothing exclusive to sign and nothing to pay to join — you can stop referring customers whenever you want.
