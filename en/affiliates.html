The six programs in the Ledyvas suite

Each program has its own job and its own price, with a monthly fee, an annual fee or a one-time payment. The commission is the same for all of them: 20%.

  • Ledyvas Analytics Métrica: The profitability of every day in restaurants, bars, hotels and excursions: recipe cost, waste, COGS % and Prime Cost from your POS sales.
  • Ledyvas Professional: The desktop ERP for operations: purchasing, inventory, recipes, production, sales and reports, with the Excursion / Tourism module.
  • Ledyvas Enterprise: Everything in Professional plus a direct API connection to QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Odoo, tax per document and accounting file export.
  • Ledyvas Ultimate: Accounting and taxes that arrive on their own: tax per document, direct API connection to QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Odoo, and DGII 606, 607 and 608 formats.
  • Ledyvas Budget & Forecast: The annual budget by account, the year-end forecast that is recalculated with each actual month, and deviations with a traffic light.
  • Ledyvas CFO: The finance director's dashboard on your accounting data: indicators, alerts and an Action Plan every month.
