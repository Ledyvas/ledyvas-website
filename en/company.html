Company

Our story

Ledyvas was born with a clear vision: to develop a modern, intuitive and efficient business management platform to help small and medium businesses organize their operations and grow sustainably.

Our goal is to simplify daily processes through a solution that brings together purchasing, sales, inventory, production, customers, suppliers, analytics and strategic information in one place.

We believe technology should adapt to businesses, not the other way around. That's why we build flexible software, ready to evolve alongside the needs of each business.

Mission

To develop business management solutions that let companies work more efficiently, make better decisions and increase productivity through a modern, stable and easy-to-use platform.

Vision

To make Ledyvas an internationally recognized platform for managing small and medium businesses, offering innovation, simplicity, trust and continuous evolution.

The origin of Ledyvas

Ledyvas is not just management software. It's the result of more than four decades of international professional experience, across different countries and business cultures, turned into a platform built for real businesses, from restaurants and food and beverage companies to shops, beauty centers and service businesses.

Every feature has been developed with a clear goal: to simplify daily work, improve control over information and contribute to the growth of each business, combining experience, innovation and continuous improvement.
