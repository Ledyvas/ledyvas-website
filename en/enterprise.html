What it is

Tourism operations and accounting, with no manual work in between

Ledyvas Enterprise is a desktop ERP built for tourism, excursion and multi-destination restaurant operations. It records the daily operation and, instead of passing data by hand to the accountant, sends it on its own to QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books or Odoo.

Everything in Professional included

Assisted purchasing A and B, inventory, recipes and production, destinations, PAX, fuel, reports and business intelligence.

Native connectors: QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Odoo

Connects to the client's accounting account over the internet and sends journal entries, invoices, expenses and payments on its own, with no file exports or imports. It can sync automatically every night.

Tax per document

On every purchase and sale you see the subtotal, the tax and the total with tax. Tax does not affect the margin.

File export

For accountants who use QuickBooks Desktop, Xero or Alegra: exports the transactions as a file, ready to import.

Margin by activity

What each excursion, destination or activity earns, with the real cost of food, beverages and fuel per PAX.

6 languages, unlimited users

Spanish, English, Italian, French, Portuguese and Arabic (right to left). Users with roles, no per-user cost. Light and dark theme, printing, PDF, screenshot, and data export and import in Excel.

Honesty

What it doesn't do

  • It does not keep full accounting or do tax closings, that is done by your accounting software and your accountant. Ledyvas feeds it.
  • It does not generate the DGII 606, 607 and 608 formats for the Dominican Republic, that is part of Ledyvas Ultimate.
  • It is not cloud-based and does not work with several PCs on a network over the same data. Each installation is standalone; a business with two locations needs two licenses.

Use cases

Examples by industry

Illustrative scenarios showing how different types of businesses can use Ledyvas.

Enterprise · United States and United Arab Emirates · USD / AED

Fine-dining restaurant with imported ingredients

For supplier invoices in several currencies (ingredients from Europe, Japan or Asia), Ledyvas handles exchange rates across the whole interface. The QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Odoo connectors send the accounting data over without retyping it.

Features: Multi-currency, accounting connectors (QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Odoo).

Enterprise · United States · USD

Tour and boat operator with QuickBooks Online

The team enters PAX, destinations, fleet and fuel in Ledyvas while the accountant works in QuickBooks Online. Purchases, sales and expenses go up through the API as bills, invoices, expenses and payments, with tax per document and no re-keying. Each document is identified by its number, so syncing twice never duplicates anything.

Features: Excursion Mode, QuickBooks Online (API), tax per document.

Enterprise · France and Spain · EUR

Catering and hospitality with Odoo

Purchasing, recipes, production and inventory run in Ledyvas. Odoo receives vendor bills and customer invoices through its API as drafts, for the accountant to review and confirm. Requires Odoo 19 with external API access.

Features: Recipes and production, Odoo (API), drafts for the accountant.
