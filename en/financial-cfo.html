Ledyvas Financial CFO

Decide with the numbers,
not with a hunch.

Ledyvas Financial CFO is the decision dashboard that sits on top of your accounting. It reads data from QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Odoo, Xero and Alegra, or from Excel/CSV files and Ledyvas programs, and turns it into an executive dashboard, alerts and a monthly Ledyvas Action Plan. Subscription, only through Official Partners. Launch rate.

What it is

The CFO's dashboard, without building a finance department

Ledyvas Financial CFO is a desktop program for companies that already keep their books in other software. It brings the numbers in, sorts them out and answers the question of every month: what is happening and what should be done. The client installs and uses it; the Partner presents and sells it and does not handle the client's money or finances.

Executive summary

Sales, EBITDA, margin, net income and cash flow on a single screen. Tap any figure to see where it comes from.

Ledyvas Action Plan

Every month, a prioritised list of actions drawn from the deviations in your figures, delivered in writing. No meetings.

Complete analysis

Profitability, costs and shrinkage, efficiency, liquidity, working capital, debt, cash flow, budget versus actual and scenarios.

Alerts

Warnings when an indicator leaves its range, such as days sales outstanding, margin or liquidity.

AI assistant, optional

Answers questions about your figures with Claude. It uses your own Anthropic key, only if you give permission, and does not send the company name.

6 languages, 156 currencies

Spanish, English, Italian, French, Portuguese and Arabic (right to left). Light and dark theme, print, PDF, screenshot and export.

Where it takes the data from

It connects to what you already use

Your accounting, through the API

Direct connection to QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Odoo, Xero and Alegra. The first import accepts up to 3 years of history.

Files

Import Excel or CSV: income statement, balance sheet, receivables and payables, banks. If the balance sheet is missing, you can type it in.

Ledyvas programs

Reads the export from Enterprise and Ultimate and the Métrica reports in Excel (P&L and Executive summary).

Honesty

What it does not do

  • It does not keep the books or do tax closings, your accounting software and your accountant do that. CFO reads that data and analyses it.
  • It is not financial, tax or legal advice: the figures and the plan support the decision, and the decision belongs to the company.
  • It does not make payments or move money: it only reads and analyses.
  • It is not cloud-based and does not work with several PCs on the same data. Each installation is standalone and its data stays on your PC.
