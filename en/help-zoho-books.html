Ledyvas + Zoho Books

Send the double-entry journals for your purchases and sales from Ledyvas straight into Zoho Books — no CSV, no manual keying. Entries land as drafts for your accountant to review.

What it does

Ledyvas is a desktop ERP for small businesses in hospitality, tourism and retail. Your team runs the day inside Ledyvas — purchases, inventory, sales, cash, fuel and fleet — and this integration pushes the accounting entries into Zoho Books through the Zoho Books API.

  • Balanced double-entry journals for every purchase and sale, created as drafts.
  • Optionally creates the vendor and customer contacts that are missing in Zoho Books.
  • No duplicates: each document is matched by its reference, both in Ledyvas and against Zoho, before anything is created.
  • Re-syncing a period is always safe.
Tax (VAT / ITBIS) is not synced. Ledyvas does not store tax separately; your accountant applies tax in Zoho Books.

Which version you need

The Zoho Books integration is part of Ledyvas Enterprise. Download it from the Enterprise trial page — 14 days free with your own data. After the trial, Ledyvas Enterprise is offered by monthly subscription, only through a Ledyvas Official Distributor. The "Sync with Zoho Books" screen appears under Quick Actions in that build.

How to connect

  1. In Ledyvas, open Quick Actions → Sync with Zoho Books.
  2. Click Connect with Zoho Books. Your browser opens Zoho's consent screen.
  3. Approve access. The browser shows "Connected", and Ledyvas finishes the setup automatically.
  4. If your Zoho account has more than one organization, choose the one to sync to.

Ledyvas never sees your Zoho password, and never stores the OAuth client secret (that lives only on the Ledyvas server). The connection can be revoked at any time from Zoho, or disconnected from inside Ledyvas.

How to sync

  1. Choose a date range (From / To).
  2. Leave "Also create missing vendors and customers" ticked if you want Ledyvas to create them in Zoho Books.
  3. Optionally open Chart of accounts and map each category to a Zoho account (see below).
  4. Click Sync now.

The result panel shows how many journals were created, how many were skipped (already there), and how many contacts were created. The three cards at the bottom — Transactions in the period / Already synced / To sync now — refresh whenever you change the date range.

Chart of accounts mapping

By default Ledyvas maps to the standard control accounts (Sales, Cost of goods sold, Other expenses, Undeposited funds) that already exist in a new Zoho Books organization, so the first sync works with no configuration.

To send a category to a specific account, open Chart of accounts, pick the account from the dropdown next to each row, and click Save chart of accounts. Any account name you choose must already exist in your Zoho Books chart of accounts. Accounts receivable / accounts payable are not offered — Zoho requires a contact per line for those, and Ledyvas models each purchase and sale as already settled against Undeposited funds (which you can later reclassify to your real bank account).

Automatic nightly sync

Tick Sync automatically every night (last 120 days). While Ledyvas is open, it runs the sync once a day on its own, covering the last 120 days. The last automatic run and how many journals it created are shown under the checkbox.

If you void or edit a document

On the next sync (manual or automatic), Ledyvas compares each already-synced document against its current state in Ledyvas:

  • Voided in Ledyvas → the matching journal in Zoho Books is deleted.
  • Edited in Ledyvas (date, total or line count changed) → the old journal is deleted and a new one is created.

The result panel reports these as "Journals rebuilt" and "Journals deleted".

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with QuickBooks, Xero, Odoo, Alegra?
Yes — through file export ("Export to Accounting"). Zoho Books is the only one with a native API sync.
I already synced this month. Is re-syncing safe?
Yes. Ledyvas checks each document reference locally and in Zoho before creating anything — no duplicates.
Where are my Zoho tokens stored?
In Ledyvas's local database on your computer, encrypted at rest. Ledyvas never stores the OAuth client secret; that lives only on the Ledyvas server.
What gets written to Zoho Books?
Only draft journal entries and (optionally) missing vendor/customer contacts. Ledyvas only reads your chart of accounts and existing journals, for the duplicate check.
Can I disconnect?
Yes, anytime, from inside Ledyvas or from your Zoho account. Journals already synced stay in Zoho Books.
Which OAuth scope does it request?
ZohoBooks.journals.ALL, ZohoBooks.contacts.ALL and ZohoBooks.settings.READ — only what the integration uses: create/read/delete manual journals, create/read contacts, and read the chart of accounts and organization list. It does not request access to invoices, expenses, banking or any other Zoho Books data.
Is this a free add-on?
The Zoho Books integration is part of Ledyvas Enterprise, available through a Ledyvas Official Distributor. It is not sold separately on the marketplace.
