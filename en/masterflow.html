Ledyvas MasterFlow

How much to order and when,

Ledyvas MasterFlow works out every day what you should buy so you don't run out of stock or overfill the storeroom. You approve, and the program builds one purchase order per supplier. Monthly fee, annual fee or one-time payment.

What it's for

Buy just enough, on time

Ledyvas MasterFlow is a Windows desktop program: it is installed on the company PC and the data stays there, encrypted. It answers three questions: what to order today, from which supplier and how much.

Daily suggestions

What you should order today. You approve, reject or change the quantity of each line.

Purchase orders

One order per supplier, numbered (PO-YYYY-0001), as PDF, Excel, a file to import into your ERP, or by email to the supplier. It tracks goods in transit and receipt.

Dashboard

Products at risk of running out, days of coverage and purchases by arrival week, in transit and planned.

Master data

Per product: purchase unit, lead time, safety stock, minimum order and case size. Imported from Excel or CSV.

Recipes

For restaurants and hotels: dish sales become ingredient consumption, with waste, and go into the purchase calculation.

Connection to your ERP

Reads products, suppliers, costs, stock and sales from Odoo, QuickBooks Online or Zoho Inventory, read-only, once a day.

How it works

A clear calculation, no black boxes

Average daily sales = sales of the last 90 days ÷ days with stock (days without stock don't count). Reorder point = average daily sales × lead time + safety stock. When stock plus goods in transit falls below that point, MasterFlow suggests an order, rounded to the supplier's case size and minimum order.

  • Import products and sales history from Excel or CSV, with templates.
  • Or connect it read-only to Odoo, QuickBooks Online or Zoho Inventory.
  • The Dashboard exports to Excel with a sheet that Ledyvas CFO can import into its cash projection.
  • Built-in AI assistant, no key of your own: it answers with your figures (uses the internet).
  • 6 languages and a demo company to see it working.
Honesty

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't keep the accounts or invoice, and it changes nothing in your ERP: it only reads.
  • It isn't cloud-based and doesn't run on several networked PCs over the same data: each license is for 1 PC.
  • It doesn't make up data: if something is missing, it doesn't calculate that line and tells you.
