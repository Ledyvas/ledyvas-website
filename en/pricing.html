How it works

From free trial to your active license

1. Free trial

Download Ledyvas and try every feature with no restrictions.

2. Buy your license

Click "Buy now" to see the marketplaces where Ledyvas is available, and buy it directly there — you choose where to buy, with no intervention from us.

3. Online activation

You'll receive your license code (format LDV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) by email. The first time you open Ledyvas, you enter it along with your email on the activation screen: the system validates it against our server and links it to that machine. If you change or lose your PC, the license can be transferred to a new machine without losing your data.

4. Customer Portal

A private area where you'll be able to check your active licenses, authorized activations, invoices, and latest downloads, all in one place. We're finishing it up.
