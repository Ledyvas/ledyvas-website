Pricing

Each program, its own price

The Ledyvas suite has six programs and each one is purchased separately: monthly fee, annual fee or one-time payment. The price is the same in every country, in US dollars (USD). Choose the program to see its price. With the one-time payment, updates are free for the first year.

Ledyvas Analytics Métrica

The profitability of every day in restaurants, bars, hotels and excursions: recipe cost, waste, COGS % and Prime Cost from your POS sales.

Ledyvas Professional
