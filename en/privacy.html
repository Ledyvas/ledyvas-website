Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Ledyvas is business management software developed and distributed by Leonardo Cosci. For any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can write to us at info@ledyvas.com.

2. What data the software handles

Ledyvas is desktop software: the information you enter into the program (customers, products, sales, purchases, etc.) is stored locally on your own computer or on the destination you choose (for example, an external drive). We do not send that information to Ledyvas servers or to third parties.

3. Data we collect ourselves

We collect the data you voluntarily provide when contacting us by email (for example, for support or to purchase a license): your name, email address and the content of your message.

In addition, this website uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (pages visited, time spent, approximate location and device type). Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect your IP address. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We are working on adding a cookie management panel so you can accept or reject this tracking.

4. Use of your contact data

We use the data you send us solely to respond to your inquiry, provide support or manage your license purchase. We do not share or sell this information to third parties.

5. Data retention

We retain email correspondence for as long as necessary to provide support and comply with reasonable administrative or legal obligations.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal data you have provided by email, by writing to info@ledyvas.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be published on this same page with the corresponding update date.
