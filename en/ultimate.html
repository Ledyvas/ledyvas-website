What it is

Operations and accounting, with no manual work in between

Ledyvas Ultimate is a desktop ERP (installed on the business PC, works offline most of the time). It records purchases, inventory, products, customers, suppliers, sales, accounts receivable and payable, and expenses. At month-end, instead of moving data by hand, it sends it straight to QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books or Odoo.

Native connectors: QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Odoo

Connects to the client's accounting account over the internet and posts journal entries, bills, expenses and payments on its own, no CSV export or import. It can sync automatically every night.

DGII 606 / 607 / 608

Generates the monthly DGII file from purchases, sales and expenses already recorded, with the NCF fields in Purchase and Sale. Ready to upload to the Virtual Office. Submission and e-invoicing are handled by your certified software (PSFE).

VAT / ITBIS per document

Every purchase and sale shows the subtotal, the tax and the total with tax, two clear figures. Tax never touches the margin.

Universal catalog

Starts neutral and loads your industry's categories in one click: retail, services, workshop, factory, beauty, distribution, hospitality. Fits any business.

Built for the accountant

Clean menu, Accounts Receivable / Payable, Petty Cash, Margin by Activity. A clear interface to review and close the month fast.

5 languages, unlimited users

Spanish, Italian, English, French and Arabic (right-to-left interface). As many users as the business needs, each with a role. No per-user cost. Light and dark theme, printing, PDF, screenshot, and data export and import in Excel. Built-in AI assistant, no API key of your own needed: it answers your questions about using the program (uses internet).

Straight talk

What it does not do

  • It does not do full accounting or the fiscal close, that's your accounting software and your accountant. Ledyvas feeds it.
  • It does not submit anything to the DGII or issue the electronic invoice (e-CF), that's the certified e-invoicing software (PSFE).
  • It is not cloud-based and does not run across several networked PCs on the same data. Each install is standalone. A business with two locations takes two licenses.
International expansion

Beyond the Dominican Republic

Ledyvas Ultimate is sold worldwide. The native QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Odoo connectors work in any country where those platforms are used; the DGII 606, 607 and 608 formats are an extra for the Dominican Republic.

United States and other countries with sales tax: Ledyvas Ultimate does not calculate tax by state. On every purchase and sale it applies the tax code you have already set up in QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books or Odoo, and that accounting system calculates the tax at the rate of your jurisdiction.

Use cases

Examples by industry

Illustrative scenarios showing how different types of businesses can use Ledyvas.

Ultimate · Italy · EUR

Food distribution center with its own fleet

Combines Fuel and Fleet with Recipes and Production: raw materials are transformed in the culinary lab while the diesel used by the vans delivering ready meals is tracked, all in one system.

Features: Fuel and Fleet + Recipes and Production.

Ultimate · Mexico · MXN

Traditional food factory and distributor

The Recipes and Production module turns flour, sugar and other raw materials into finished goods while controlling work-in-progress inventory. Turning off the excursion modules simplifies the menu, so the warehouse team can focus on purchasing, the logistics center and waste.

Features: Recipes and Production, work-in-progress inventory, waste, turning modules on/off.

Ultimate · Dominican Republic (Bávaro) · DOP

Service station and tourist fleet

The Fuel and Fleet module brings daily mileage, storage tanks and performance per vehicle or vessel into a single screen. NCF support for the DGII (606 and 607 formats) automates paperwork that used to take a week.

Features: Fuel and Fleet, NCF DGII 606/607.

Ultimate · Dominican Republic (including foreign-owned operations) · DOP

Restaurants and catering: Dominican tax regime

The Dominican Republic tax regime is set up in minutes. The DGII Reports screen generates the 606, 607 and 608 files as required by regulation, and ITBIS is calculated automatically as purchases and sales are entered, without affecting the margin. Features: DGII Reports 606/607/608, ITBIS, NCF.
