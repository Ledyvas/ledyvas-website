Ledyvas Ultimate

The ERP that talks
to your accounting.

Ledyvas Ultimate runs your day-to-day operation and connects it natively to Zoho Books. It handles VAT / ITBIS per document and generates the DGII 606, 607 and 608 formats for the Dominican Republic. Built for businesses that work hand in hand with their accountant.

What it is

Operations and accounting, with no manual work in between

Ledyvas Ultimate is a desktop ERP (installed on the business PC, works offline most of the time). It records purchases, inventory, products, customers, suppliers, sales, accounts receivable and payable, and expenses. At month-end, instead of moving data by hand, it sends it straight to Zoho Books.

Native Zoho Books connector

Connects to the client's Zoho Books account over the internet and posts journal entries, bills, expenses and payments on its own — no CSV export or import. It can sync automatically every night.

DGII 606 / 607 / 608

Generates the monthly DGII file from purchases, sales and expenses already recorded, with the NCF fields in Purchase and Sale. Ready to upload to the Virtual Office. Submission and e-invoicing are handled by your certified software (PSFE).

VAT / ITBIS per document

Every purchase and sale shows the subtotal, the tax and the total with tax — two clear figures. Tax never touches the margin.

Universal catalog

Starts neutral and loads your industry's categories in one click: retail, services, workshop, factory, beauty, distribution, hospitality. Fits any business.

Built for the accountant

Clean menu, Accounts Receivable / Payable, Petty Cash, Margin by Activity. A clear interface to review and close the month fast.

5 languages, unlimited users

Spanish, Italian, English, French and Arabic (right-to-left interface). As many users as the business needs, each with a role. No per-user cost.

Straight talk

What it does not do

  • It does not do full accounting or the fiscal close — that's Zoho Books and your accountant. Ledyvas feeds Zoho.
  • It does not submit anything to the DGII or issue the electronic invoice (e-CF) — that's the certified e-invoicing software (PSFE).
  • It is not cloud-based and does not run across several networked PCs on the same data. Each install is standalone. A business with two locations takes two licenses.
