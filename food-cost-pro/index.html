Frequently asked questions

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The 14-day trial starts with one click and asks for no payment details.

Does it work with invoices from my country?

The AI reads invoices in any language and currency. You review every line before saving, so you always have the final say.

Where is my data kept?

In your Food Cost Pro account, on Cloudflare servers. Only you can see it, and you can export it to Excel at any time.

Does it work for several locations?

The app handles several locations, each with its own supplier prices. The web plan includes 1 location.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your data stays saved. To keep working, activate your Food Cost Pro licence.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in the browser on your phone and PC, and it can be installed as an app from the browser, without an app store.
